## 1. Thermocouple – Calculate the resulting EMF

Given:

- \(c = 3.75 \times 10^{-2}\text{ mV/°C}\)
    
- \(k = 4.5 \times 10^{-5}\text{ mV/°C}^2\)
    
- \(T_1 = 100°C\) (hot junction)
    
- \(T_2 = 0°C\) (cold junction, ice)
    

Formula:

\[ E=c(T_1-T_2)+k(T_1^2-T_2^2) \]

Solution:

Substitute the given values:

\[ E=0.0375(100-0)+0.000045(100^2-0^2) \]

\[ E=3.75+0.45 \]

\[ \boxed{E=4.20\text{ mV}} \]

Final Answer: 4.20 mV

## 2. Resistance Type Sensor – Calculate resistance at 50°C

Given:

- \(R_o=150\ \Omega\)
    
- Reference temperature \(=20°C\)
    
- Operating temperature \(=50°C\)
    
- \(\alpha_{20}=0.00392\)
    

Formula:

$R=R_o(1+\alpha\Delta T)$

First, calculate the temperature difference:

$\Delta T=50-20=30°C$

Substitute:

$R=150(1+0.00392(30))$

$R=150(1+0.1176)$

$R=150(1.1176)$

 $\boxed{R=167.64\ \Omega}$

Final Answer: 167.64 Ω

## 3. Venturi Flowmeter – Calculate volume flow rate

Given:

- Differential pressure: \(\Delta P=16\text{ kPa}\)
    
- Meter constant: \(k=0.75\)
    

The formula appears in your notes as:

$Q=k\sqrt{\Delta P}$

Solution:

Substitute the given values:

$Q=0.75\sqrt{16}$

$Q=0.75(4)$

$\boxed{Q=3}$
Final Answer: 3 (flow-rate units)

Note: The square-root symbol may have been lost during text extraction. This solution assumes the intended formula is \(Q=k\sqrt{\Delta P}\), which is the usual relationship for differential-pressure flowmeters.
